How to update (free)

  1. Download the new version: Etsy → You → Purchases, or the file from the update email.
  2. Replace the BudgetDrop .html file in your BudgetDrop folder with the new one. Leave BudgetDrop data.json where it is.
  3. Open it. If asked, click Reconnect or Open my data file. Your budget is exactly as you left it.
